Complete Series Finder

Upload a UTF-8 text file containing your bookshelf entries. The app counts each book token and returns series whose observed count matches the number at the end of the series name (for example, experiment-x-2).

Lines may contain comma-separated or space-separated entries. Shelf labels such as to-read and currently-reading are ignored. Maximum upload size: 2 MiB.
